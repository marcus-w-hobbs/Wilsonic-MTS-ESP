# Quick Start

I want you to hear one of Erv Wilson's scales within a minute of installing,
so this page is the shortest path to sound. Everything here uses the built-in
"simple synth," which I include so you can audition scales and confirm your
other synths are in tune. It is deliberately basic, and the real payoff comes
later, when Wilsonic retunes the synths you already love inside your DAW.

## 1. Open the right app

The installer puts **two** applications on your machine, and their names
differ by one word:

- **Wilsonic** — has the built-in simple synth. This is the one that makes
  sound by itself.
- **WilsonicController** — a MIDI effect for driving other instruments. It
  makes **no sound on its own**, by design.

Open **Wilsonic**, the one *without* "Controller" in the name. If you want
the full story on the difference, read
[Which Wilsonic Is Which](which-wilsonic.md).

## 2. Play the keyboard

Click the colored keyboard along the bottom of the window with your mouse or
trackpad. Each key you click plays through the simple synth. On a
touchscreen you can play the keys directly.

If you have a MIDI keyboard attached, open **Options → Audio/MIDI Settings**
and enable it as a MIDI input, and then it will play the simple synth too.

## 3. If you hear nothing

- Confirm you opened **Wilsonic**, not **WilsonicController**.
- Open **Options → Audio/MIDI Settings** and check that your audio output
  device is selected.
- Check your system output volume.

If it is still silent after that, please tell me about it on
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/marcus-w-hobbs/Wilsonic-MTS-ESP/issues)
with your OS version and what you tried, because that likely means I have a
bug or another gap in this manual.

## 4. Pick a scale

1. Open the **Scale Design** menu at the top left.
2. Choose **Wilson's Garden**.
3. Step through the **Curated Presets** menu and play as you go. These are
   scales curated from Erv Wilson's designs, and it is the best first tour
   of what the app is about.

## 5. Where to go next

The simple synth is a reference tone. The reason Wilsonic exists is to
retune your *other* synths — every MTS-ESP-aware synth in your DAW follows
Wilsonic's tuning in real time, with no routing or setup. That is covered in
[MTS-ESP and Your DAW](daw-setup.md). If you do not have a synth that
supports MTS-ESP yet, [Surge XT](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/) is
free, sounds great, and supports it natively.
